Instructions for Blood Donors

Before Donation
  • Donor must be between 18–60 years of age.
  • Minimum weight should be 50 kg.
  • Have a healthy meal and drink plenty of water.
  • Avoid alcohol for at least 24 hours.
  • Get adequate sleep before donating.
During & After Donation
  • Relax and stay calm during the donation process.
  • The donation takes about 10–15 minutes.
  • Rest for at least 15 minutes after donation.
  • Drink fluids and avoid heavy exercise for the day.
  • Next donation is allowed after 3 months.
Donors with fever, recent surgery, infections, or chronic illnesses should consult medical staff before donating blood.
